Biological Filtration Science

The 30-Day Planted Tank Nitrogen Cycle

Active aquasoils release ammonia during the first two weeks. Follow this 4-stage biological maturation protocol before introducing sensitive Discus or Caridina/Neocaridina shrimp.

Days 1 – 7 • Stage 01

Ammonia Leaching (NH₃/NH₄⁺)

Active aquasoil releases initial ammonia (2–4 ppm). Perform 50% water changes every 2 days and dose concentrated nitrifying bacteria starter. Keep lighting at 6 hours/day.

Days 8 – 16 • Stage 02

Nitrite Spike (NO₂⁻)

Nitrosomonas bacteria oxidize ammonia into Nitrite. Diatom brown algae may appear on glass. Perform two 40% water changes per week.

Days 17 – 25 • Stage 03

Nitrate Conversion (NO₃⁻)

Nitrobacter colonies establish inside your biological filter media. Ammonia and Nitrite drop to 0.0 ppm while plants actively pearl oxygen.

Day 26+ • Stage 04

Livestock Introduction

Introduce Clean-Up Crew (Neocaridina Shrimp & Nerite Snails) first, followed 7 days later by quarantined Discus, Betta, or Guppies via slow drip acclimation.
